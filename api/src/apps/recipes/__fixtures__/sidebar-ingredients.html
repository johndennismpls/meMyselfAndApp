Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups buttermilk

These pancakes are tall, tender, and take ten minutes.

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Pour in the buttermilk and stir until just combined; lumps are fine.
  3. Cook on a buttered griddle over medium heat, about 2 minutes a side.
